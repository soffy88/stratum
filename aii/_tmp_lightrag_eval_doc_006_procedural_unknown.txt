# Gaussian Asset Pricing Model with Quadratic Utility

Type: procedural | Subject: unknown

Let the state variable $ s_t $ follow a Gaussian Markov process:
$$
s_{t+1} = A s_t + C w_{t+1}, \quad w_{t+1} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I),
$$
where $ A $ is a matrix with spectral radius $ \leq 1/\sqrt{\beta} $, $ C $ is a covariance matrix.
The representative consumer has quadratic utility:
$$
u(c_t) = -\frac{1}{2}(c_t - b)^2, \quad b > 0.
$$
The equilibrium consumption process $ \{c_t\} $ is linear in $ s_t $:
$$
c_t = S_c s_t.
$$
Asset prices are derived from the inner product of discounted marginal utility and dividend processes (see HOW for details).
The marginal cost of business cycles is linked to the covariance between consumption and marginal utility:
$$
\text{Cov}_{t-1}(c_t, m_t) < 0 \impl \text{Risk Premium} = - \frac{\text{Cov}_{t-1}(c_t, m_t)}{E_{t-1}[m_t c_t]}.
$$
