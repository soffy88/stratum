# Summary: 04_教辅_2020春季《课堂作业》数学8年级下册答案

This document is a comprehensive answer key for a Grade 8 second-semester math workbook. It provides solutions and final answers for chapter exercises, chapter reviews, and test review problems across several major topics in junior high mathematics.

## Main coverage

### Chapter 16: [[concepts/double-radical]]
- Focuses on [[concepts/double-radical]] operations, simplification, comparison, addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, and constraints on valid values.
- Includes many worked solutions using algebraic manipulation, rationalization, and order comparison.
- Emphasizes that expressions under square roots must satisfy domain conditions.

### Chapter 17: [[concepts/pythagorean-theorem]]
- Covers the [[concepts/pythagorean-theorem]], its converse, and applications in triangles, quadrilaterals, and coordinate settings.
- Includes proofs of right triangles, distances, and geometric constructions.
- Uses the theorem to solve area, length, and angle problems, including some multi-step proof problems.

### Chapter 18: parallelogram and special quadrilaterals
- Systematically reviews properties and criteria of parallelograms.
- Extends to rectangle, rhombus, and square.
- Includes proof-based problems involving diagonals, midpoints, perpendicularity, congruence, and area-bisecting lines.
- Highlights how different special quadrilaterals can be identified from side, angle, diagonal, and symmetry conditions.

### Chapter 19: [[concepts/linear-function]]
- Covers variables and functions, graph interpretation, [[concepts/linear-function]]s, proportional functions, and function applications.
- Includes graph-based reasoning, determining formulas from points, translation of graphs, and piecewise linear contexts such as pricing, travel, fuel consumption, and production planning.
- Also addresses the relationship between [[concepts/linear-function]]s and equations/inequalities.

### Chapter 20: statistics
- Focuses on statistics topics including mean, median, mode, and variation.
- Compares datasets using variance, stability, and overall distribution shape.
- Uses data interpretation in practical settings such as contests, sports evaluation, classroom performance, heights, household waste, and industrial output.
- Reinforces that statistical conclusions depend on the chosen indicator and the context of comparison.

## Notable ideas and patterns
- Many answers are given with short proof sketches rather than full derivations, reflecting the workbook-answer format.
- Geometry sections rely heavily on auxiliary constructions such as perpendiculars, diagonals, and midpoints.
- Function problems often convert real-world descriptions into piecewise expressions and then compare intervals.
- Statistics problems contrast average performance with dispersion, showing that a higher mean does not always imply better or more stable performance.

## Overall value
This document serves as a dense reference of final answers for an entire workbook semester review. It is especially useful for checking solution correctness, identifying common problem types, and seeing standard methods for [[concepts/double-radical]], [[concepts/pythagorean-theorem]], parallelogram, [[concepts/linear-function]], and statistics.
