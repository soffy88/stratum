# Grade 7 English Writing Models

[[concepts/grade-7-english-writing-models]] collects the reference compositions provided in [[summaries/05_教辅_2020春季《课堂作业》英语7年级下册答案]] and shows the main writing patterns used in Grade 7 English workbook tasks.

## What this concept covers
The source document includes multiple model essays that students can imitate for common school writing prompts. These models are not creative writing pieces; they are practical templates for exam and homework use.

Typical purposes include:
- self-introduction
- describing a friend or classmate
- explaining daily routines and habits
- giving directions
- describing weather and vacations
- recounting trips and weekend activities
- introducing school rules or family rules
- writing about food, meals, and celebrations

## Core writing themes from the source
The reference compositions in the workbook answer key focus on recurring Grade 7 topics:

### 1. Personal introduction and friendship
Students learn how to introduce:
- name, age, nationality, school
- appearance and personality
- talents and hobbies
- reasons why someone is interesting or likable

This pattern appears in the model about Jack and in the pen-pal style introduction.

### 2. Daily life and routines
Several models describe a normal day, such as:
- getting up early
- taking exercise
- going to school
- practicing sports
- eating healthy food
- going to bed on time

This type of writing connects strongly with daily routines.

### 3. School rules and home rules
The source also includes a letter explaining rules at school or at home. Common sentence structures are:
- Don’t arrive late.
- Please keep the classroom clean.
- I have to...
- I can’t...

This overlaps with school rules.

### 4. Transportation and commuting
One composition explains how different students get to school and how long it takes. It uses:
- walk
- ride a bike
- take the subway
- drive
- far from / close to

This aligns with transportation.

### 5. Weather and seasonal scenes
The workbook includes descriptive letters about vacations and weather, using expressions like:
- sunny and hot
- cool wind
- raining
- having great fun
- enjoying the beach

This connects with weather and weather expressions.

### 6. Places, directions, and neighborhood descriptions
A model invites a friend to a birthday party and explains the route to a home. It uses:
- turn right / turn left
- walk along...
- across from
- next to
- community / neighborhood

This overlaps with places and directions.

### 7. Animals and zoo guide writing
The zoo guide composition describes animals by origin, appearance, habits, and safety advice. It includes:
- koalas, elephants, lions
- from Australia / Thailand / South Africa
- friendly, smart, shy, scary
- sleep during the day
- stay away from dangerous animals

This reflects the animal-description style in animals.

### 8. Food, meals, and birthday customs
The workbook also models writing about meals and celebrations:
- breakfast, lunch, dinner
- vegetables, meat, noodles, porridge
- birthday cakes and long noodles
- food as part of health and tradition

This connects with food and ordering.

### 9. Travel memories and school trips
Several essays describe school trips and vacations by telling:
- where the trip happened
- what transportation was used
- what activities were done
- what the writer learned or felt

These compositions are useful for past events and travel.

## Writing style in the models
The models share several important features:

- **Simple sentence structures** suitable for Grade 7 learners
- **Clear logical order**: introduction, body, closing
- **Everyday vocabulary** instead of advanced expressions
- **Useful linking words** such as now, then, after that, but, because, and
- **Exam-friendly phrasing** that matches workbook and test prompts

## Practical value
These writing models help students:
- memorize standard paragraph patterns
- learn how to answer writing prompts directly
- use correct tense and grammar in context
- improve organization and coherence
- prepare for schoolwork and exam writing sections
